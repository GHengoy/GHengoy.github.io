SimpleTrackers

Plain-English guides and simple tracking routines for three everyday projects: caring for several cats, producing a podcast, and teaching yourself Irish.

Each topic has its own site with in-depth, practical articles. The guides answer the questions people actually search for, such as how many litter boxes multiple cats need, how often to publish a podcast, or how long it takes to learn Irish, and show how a few minutes of record-keeping makes the answer clearer. There are currently 41 guides, and new ones are added regularly.

How these guides are written

  • Practical first. Every guide starts by answering its question directly, then explains the reasoning.
  • No medical or professional claims. The cat guides help you observe and record; decisions about your cats' health belong with your veterinarian.
  • Kept up to date. Guides are revised when we find a clearer way to explain something or a reader points out a problem.
